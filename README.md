# AI Adoption Intel
The Data pipeline that collects the data from U.S. Census Bureau’s Business Trends and Outlook Survey and stores it in PostgreSQL for analysis among the U.S. firms using the AI in their business from November 3rd 2025 to September 7th  2026.
## Why it matters
Tracking the AI adoption in these firms helps people, mainly for business persons understand how fast they are changing from normal work culture to these AI culture.
## Key Findings
From November 3 2025 to September 7 2026 the use AI in these firms rose from 17.3% to 23.8% increase in 6.5 or 37.6% rise.
